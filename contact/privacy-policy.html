Contact privacy · updated 4 October 2026

Your inquiry details.

nioquant handles the details you share in a contact inquiry. For questions about those details, contact info@nioquant.com .

What your browser saves

Your inquiry list stores only the IDs of selected packages or concepts and your licence preference in your browser’s local storage. Your name, email address and message are not saved in local storage. You can remove list items on the contact page, or clear the site’s browser storage.

What an inquiry contains

An online inquiry contains your name, email address, message and selected items. We use this information to respond and discuss a potential project. If you email us directly, your email provider handles sending that message.

Online sending

Online inquiries are processed by a Cloudflare Worker and delivered through Resend to info@nioquant.com. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to protect the form from automated submissions. Your data is used to handle the inquiry, not to subscribe you to marketing.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits, navigation, app download links, selected services and design concepts, and the progress of contact inquiries. Website events include public item IDs and licence preferences. Your name, email address, message, verification token and the contents of the email preview are not included in our analytics events. This website tracking is separate from the behavior of the apps described in their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of the details you have shared, or withdraw permission for further inquiry correspondence. Details needed for an agreed project or required records may need to be retained separately.
