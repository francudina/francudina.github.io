3D Time

Bold 3D numerals, rich data at a glance

Watch Face 3D Time Customizable Data Fields 70+ Devices

Most watch faces make you squint. 3D Time doesn't. Large, dimensional numerals dominate the screen while heart rate, battery, steps, weather, and more fill in around them. Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Time with depth

Numbers that stand out

The time is always the focal point. Bold 3D-style numerals fill the display with a sense of depth that flat watch faces simply don't have. Glanceable from across the room, readable in any light.

Data, on demand

Four fields, zero clutter

Pack in what matters: heart rate, body battery, stress, steps, calories, floors, active minutes, recovery time, training status, VO2 max, weather, and more. Each position configures independently - show everything or keep it minimal.

Your colors

Make it completely yours

Change the time color, date color, background, and accent colors independently. Switch between 12h and 24h formats, choose your temperature unit, and dial in every detail to match your style.

Minimal layout

Time and nothing else

Strip it back to just the essentials - hours, minutes, seconds, and the day. No data fields, no distractions. Pure 3D time, front and center.

Built for Garmin, built to last

No clutter, no ads, no subscriptions. 3D Time works on 70+ Garmin devices, from Fenix to Forerunner to Venu, and stays out of the way while keeping everything you need within a glance.
