Game

TicTacToe

Classic game, right on your wrist

Get it on Connect IQ Privacy Policy What's new
Single Player Multiplayer ANT+ Sync Stats Tracking

Waiting for someone, stuck in a queue, or just need two minutes to yourself. TicTacToe on your Garmin watch is always ready. No phone, no setup, just tap and play.

Get started

Pick your game in seconds

Host a game and share a pairing code with a friend, join someone else's match, or play solo against the watch. Three taps and you're in.

Play anywhere

Two watches, one game

Challenge a friend with another Garmin watch. Moves sync instantly over ANT+ wireless, no internet needed. A green ring shows whose turn it is, so there's never any confusion. Or play solo and see if you can outsmart the AI.

Keep score

Track your record

Every game counts. Your wins, losses, draws, and win percentage are saved on the watch, split between single player and multiplayer. See exactly how you're doing over time without keeping track in your head.

Simple, fast, always with you

No accounts, no loading screens, no ads. Just the game you already know how to play, built for a round watch face. Works on 70+ Garmin models.
