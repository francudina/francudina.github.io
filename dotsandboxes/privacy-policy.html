Privacy Policy

Dots and Boxes

Effective date: March 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Dots and Boxes ("the App"), developed and operated by Nioquant, vl. Fran Čudina ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information. Dots and Boxes is a classic board game available on the Garmin Connect IQ platform, featuring single-player with AI, local two-player, and wireless multiplayer modes with variable board sizes.

Data We Collect

The App collects minimal data, limited to:

Game Statistics

  • Wins, losses, and draws for each game mode (AI, local, multiplayer)
  • Session statistics during active gameplay

Device Information

  • Battery level and system time (used only to generate a random pairing code for multiplayer)

How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App:

  • Displaying your game statistics (win/loss/draw record)
  • Generating a random pairing code to connect with another player via ANT wireless
  • Synchronizing game state between two watches during multiplayer

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your Garmin watch using the device's built-in storage. No data is transmitted to any external server or cloud service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Specifically:

  • No third-party analytics or tracking services are used
  • No crash reporting is sent to external services
  • No advertising networks receive your data
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • ANT wireless multiplayer is direct device-to-device communication only

Multiplayer Communication

The App uses ANT wireless protocol for multiplayer gameplay. This is a short-range, direct device-to-device radio connection between two Garmin watches. No internet connection is required, and no game data passes through any external server.

Sensors and Permissions

The App requests only the ANT permission for wireless multiplayer. It does not access GPS, heart rate, accelerometer, or any other sensor on your device.

Third-Party Services

Dots and Boxes runs on the Garmin Connect IQ platform. Your use of a Garmin device is subject to Garmin's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for data collected by Garmin independently of our App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at info@nioquant.com.
